Privacy policy

rclone-archive is a personal backup tool. It uses Google Drive access only to upload, list and read files in the owner's own Google Drive.

No data is collected, stored on third-party servers, sold or shared. Access tokens stay on the owner's computer.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
